Add a voice

Put a folder with one .safetensors (or .pth) file and its .index file into the voices folder, restart the app, and pick it under Voice. Only use voices you have the rights to.

Use it in Discord or a game

  1. Switch the voice changer on with the round button.
  2. In Discord (Settings, Voice & Video) or your game, choose CABLE Output (VB-Audio Virtual Cable) as the microphone.
  3. In Discord, turn off its noise suppression and echo cancellation so it does not alter the new voice.

If something is off

  • Friends hear nothing: check the button is green and the app or game uses CABLE Output as its microphone.
  • Voice stutters: on the Voice tab raise Chunk, or lower Context, until "Processing per chunk" is green.
  • Fan or keyboard is heard: on the Voice tab press "Calibrate to room noise" and stay quiet.
  • Your PC's sound went silent after setup: Windows made the virtual cable the default speaker. In Windows Sound settings choose your headphones as the output again.
