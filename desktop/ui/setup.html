RVC Voice

Change your voice live for games and Discord. Everything runs on this PC.

Looking for your graphics card…
Used to play a test of your new voice. Headphones are best.

What gets installed

  • The AI voice software and its base files: about 6 GB to download, 12 GB of free disk space.
  • VB-Cable, a free virtual audio cable by VB-Audio. It carries your new voice into games and Discord. Windows may ask you to confirm the driver: click Install.
  • One voice (Narrator). You can add more later.
It takes about 10 to 20 minutes on a fast connection, once. Later starts take under a minute.
